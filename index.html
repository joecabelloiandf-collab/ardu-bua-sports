About Ardú Bua Sports

Helping Sport Organisations Rise, Grow and Succeed.

Ardú means rise or elevate. Bua means victory or success. Together, Ardú Bua Sports represents a commitment to elevating sport and delivering practical results.

Our Purpose

Ardú Bua Sports is a sports management organisation dedicated to supporting public and private organisations in the planning, development and delivery of high-quality sport initiatives.

We combine practical sports management experience with strategic planning, operational support and a clear understanding of how sport can serve communities, organisations and athletes.

Privacy Policy

How we handle information when you visit our website or get in touch.

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Ardú Bua Sports is an Ireland-based sports management organisation. We respect your privacy and aim to handle personal data responsibly, in accordance with applicable data protection law, including the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Irish Data Protection Act 2018.

1. Who is responsible for your information?

For personal information received in connection with Ardú Bua Sports enquiries, Ardú Bua Sports is the organisation to contact. For questions about this notice or to exercise your data protection rights, email ardubua@gmail.com.

2. What information may we collect?

  • Information you choose to provide: your name, organisation, email address, enquiry topic, message and anything else you include in an email to us.
  • Correspondence: communications and information relevant to responding to enquiries and discussing potential projects.
  • Technical information: our hosting provider and external image providers may receive limited technical information, such as an IP address, browser information and access times, as part of delivering and securing the website.

3. How the enquiry form works

The website currently uses an email-based enquiry form. Selecting “Send Enquiry” prepares a message in your own email application addressed to ardubua@gmail.com. You must review and send that email yourself. The website does not currently store the enquiry in a separate website database or send it from a website server.

4. Why we use personal information

We use information to respond to questions, discuss potential services or partnerships, follow up on requests, administer business correspondence and protect the reliability and security of the website. Depending on the circumstances, the lawful basis may be taking steps at your request before entering a contract (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)), our legitimate interests in managing enquiries and operating the website (Article 6(1)(f)), or compliance with a legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c)).

5. Who may receive information?

Enquiry emails are handled using our email service. The website is hosted using Cloudflare infrastructure and includes photographs supplied by external websites. Those providers may process technical information needed to provide their services. We may also share relevant information with professional advisers or authorities where reasonably necessary or legally required. We do not sell the personal information you send us.

6. Cookies and external resources

The current website does not include a deliberately installed advertising or analytics tracking script. Hosting and security services may use technologies required to operate and protect the website. Some photographs are loaded from third-party servers, so requests to those servers may involve technical data. If we introduce non-essential cookies or similar tracking tools in future, we will update this notice and provide the relevant consent controls where required.

7. How long do we keep information?

Enquiries and related correspondence are kept only as long as reasonably necessary to respond, manage any follow-up, maintain relevant business records or meet applicable legal obligations. Retention is reviewed in light of the purpose and nature of the communication.

8. International transfers

Some technology or email providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. Where data protection law requires safeguards for such transfers, the appropriate mechanisms should apply through those providers.

9. Your rights

Subject to applicable conditions, you may request access to your personal information, correction, erasure, restriction, portability, or object to processing based on legitimate interests. You can contact us at ardubua@gmail.com to make a request. You may also complain to the Irish Data Protection Commission.

We may revise this notice as our services or website change. The latest version and its update date will be published here.
